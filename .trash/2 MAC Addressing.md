
> [!tip]- MAC Addressing
>  
> - A **MAC (Media Access Control) Address** is a unique hardware identifier assigned to a network interface card (NIC).  
> - It operates at the **Data Link Layer (Layer 2)** of the OSI model and is used for **local addressing** within the same network segment. 
> 
>---
>
>  
> | **Attribute**       | **Detail**                                      |  
> |---------------------|--------------------------------------------------|  
> | **Purpose**         | Identifies devices at Layer 2 of OSI            |  
> | **Format**          | 48-bit (6 bytes), usually shown in hex format   |  
> | **Scope**           | Works within a local area network (LAN)         |  
> | **Assigned By**     | Device manufacturer (burned into hardware)      |  
> | **Changeable?**     | Can be changed (spoofed) via software            |
