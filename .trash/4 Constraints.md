

> [!tip] Types of Constraints
> | **Constraint Type**            | **Description** |
> |--------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
> | **Key Constraints**            | Super Key, Candidate Key, Primary Key, Unique |
> | **Entity Integrity**           | Primary Key cannot be NULL |
> | **Referential Integrity**      | Foreign Key must match a Primary Key or be NULL |
> | **Domain Constraints**         | Restricts attribute values (e.g., Age ≥ 18) |
